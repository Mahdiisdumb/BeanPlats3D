# BeanPlats3D

**BeanPlats3D** — a 3D platformer with tragic bean lore and intentionally catastrophic endings.  

You are **the last surviving bean**. Everyone else is dead. Your only way to reproduce your kind: the **light**.  

### ⚠️ The Light Is Deadly
- The light is always far — a tempting goal  
- **Touching it crashes the game** — yes, the crash is intentional  
- Your death **splits into 1000 new beans**, carrying on your legacy  

> Platforming is your journey. The light is your doom. Survival is optional. Hilarity is guaranteed.
## Gameplay

- **Platformer Mechanics:** Jump, dodge hazards, navigate levels  
- **Objective:** Explore, survive, and optionally reach the light  
- **Lore Mechanic:** Touch the light → crash → spawn 1000 beans  
- **Challenge:** The light looks like a goal, but it’s also death
